# 03.3 ZP Credits: From "Points" to Real World Assets (RWA)

**Focus:** Utility & Liquidity  
**Key Concept:** Data as a Programmable Medium of Exchange

---

## Beyond "Loyalty Points"

In the Web2 era, "points" were liabilities on a corporate balance sheet—marketing fluff that could be devalued or expired at a whim.

In the Zusei ecosystem, **ZP (Zone Power)** credits are **Real World Assets (RWA).**
They are not backed by a marketing budget; they are backed by the **Revenue of the Data Network.** Every ZP in circulation represents a verified unit of "Ground Truth" data that an Enterprise Client (AI Agent, Hedge Fund, or Retail Brand) has paid to access.

This creates a new asset class: **Liquid Data Value.**

---

## 1. The Retail Loop (Immediate Utility)
**Status:** Live in Genesis Phase (Singapore)

The most immediate utility of ZP is within the retail network itself. Because merchants drive the demand for these credits (by burning $ZST to mint them), they accept them as valid payment to drive further traffic.

* **Direct Offsets (Cash-Equivalent):** Users can burn ZP at the Point of Sale (POS) to offset the cost of goods.
    * *Example:* A S$6.00 latte can be paid for with **50 ZP**.
* **Dynamic "Surge" Pricing:** Smart merchants can adjust the ZP-Price of goods based on their real-time need for data/traffic.
    * *Scenario:* It’s 2 PM and the shop is empty. The merchant drops the price of a coffee to **25 ZP** to attract verified footfall (miners) to the location.

---

## 2. The Mobility Bridge (EV Charging)
**Status:** Roadmap Phase 2 (2027)

As Singapore transitions to a 100% EV fleet, access to energy is the new "fuel." Zusei bridges the gap between **Retail Footfall** and **Mobility**.

* **The Partnership Model:** API integrations with major charging networks (e.g., Charge+, Tesla Superchargers).
* **The Use Case:** "Shop to Charge."
    * A user spends 1 hour working at a Zusei-verified cafe (Mining Data).
    * They earn **100 ZP** for their verified presence.
    * They plug these credits directly into the charging station app to pay for **5kWh** of EV charging.

This turns every retail stop into a "refueling" station for the user's digital wallet.

---

## 3. The Grid Offset (Utility Bills)
**Status:** Roadmap Phase 3 (2028)

The ultimate vision of Zusei is to allow ZP to interact with the national energy grid.

* **Renewable Energy Certificates (RECs):** We treat verified ZP as a micro-REC (Proof that energy was consumed efficiently by a human).
* **Bill Reduction:** By partnering with "Green Retailers" (like Geneco or SP Group), users link their Zusei wallet to their utility account.
    * *The Vision:* Use the credits earned from visiting efficient businesses to pay down your own home electricity bill.
