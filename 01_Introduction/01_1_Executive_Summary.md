# 01.1 Executive Summary

**Read Time:** 2 Minutes  
**Status:** Live Development (Singapore Pilot)

---

## The Thesis: Scarcity in the Age of Abundance

The year is 2026. The arrival of Artificial General Intelligence (AGI) has fundamentally crashed the cost of digital labor. Content, code, and "digital engagement" are now effectively infinite and free.

In a world of infinite digital abundance, value flees to the only thing that cannot be faked, printed, or generated by a server farm: **Physical Reality.**

**Zusei** is the first decentralized protocol built to capture this value. We are a **DePIN (Decentralized Physical Infrastructure Network)** that verifies human presence. While the internet drowns in bot traffic, Zusei provides the undeniable proof that a real human is standing in a real location, consuming real energy.

We are not just a check-in app. We are the operating system for the **Human Energy Grid.**

---

## The Problem: The "Empty Room" Crisis

Retail and F&B merchants are currently fighting a losing battle on two fronts:

1.  ** The Digital Trust Deficit:** Merchants spend billions on digital ads, yet over 70% of web traffic is now bot-driven. They are paying for "eyeballs" that don't exist.
2.  **The Energy Inefficiency Gap:** A cafe in Singapore pays the same high electricity bill whether the shop is empty or full. An empty room at 3 PM is not just a loss of revenue—it is a "leak" of energy (Watts) that yields zero utility.

Merchants need a way to stop paying for fake digital clicks and start optimizing for **verified physical footfall.**

---

## The Solution: Proof of Presence

Zusei introduces a new consensus mechanism: **Proof of Presence (PoP).**

By installing a lightweight IoT node (or using our verified QR protocol), merchants turn their physical stores into "Energy Mining Hubs."
* **For Users:** You earn **ZP Credits** simply by showing up. Your physical presence validates the merchant's energy usage, turning your time into a tradable asset.
* **For Merchants:** You get verified, high-intent footfall. More importantly, you gain access to **Energy Efficiency per Visitor (EEV)** data, allowing you to optimize your operations and offset costs using the Zusei network.

---

## The Market: Why Singapore? Why Now?

We are launching our Genesis Phase in **Singapore**—the perfect sandbox for a DePIN energy network.
* **High Density:** Over 20,000 F&B outlets in a compact urban grid.
* **High Energy Stakes:** With electricity tariffs rising and carbon taxes hitting S$45/tonne, merchants are desperate for efficiency.
* **Tech-Forward Populace:** A citizenry already comfortable with QR-based digital interactions.

Our goal is to onboard **500 Genesis Merchants** in the next 12 months, creating a dense "Energy Grid" of cafes, retail stores, and eventually, EV charging stations.

---

## The Business Model: The "Watt" Economy

Zusei operates on a **Burn-and-Mint Equilibrium (BME)** to ensure long-term sustainability:
1.  **Merchants** purchase and burn the governance token (**$ZST**) to access the network.
2.  **The Network** mints utility credits (**ZP**) to reward Users for verified visits.
3.  **Users** redeem ZPs for real-world value (discounts, EV charging, or bill offsets).

This circular economy ensures that as the network grows, the supply of $ZST is naturally constricted, while the utility of the network scales with physical usage.

## Conclusion

We are moving from an economy of "Likes" to an economy of **Watts**. Zusei is the bridge that allows physical businesses to survive and thrive in the post-AGI era.

**We don't just track where people are. We power where they go.**
